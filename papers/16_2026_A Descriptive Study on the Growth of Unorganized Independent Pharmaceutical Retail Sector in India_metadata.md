## Abstract / Description
For over a century, the Indian pharmaceutical retail market has been dominated by the unorganized pharmaceutical retail sector, which not only makes essential drugs available to the huge diversified population of India but also provides millions of jobs. Presently, these unorganized pharmacies are facing tremendous challenges in attractive prices and the large variety of products offered by organized and e-pharmacies. The survival of these independent pharmacies is of prime concern in this research. A field survey of Indian unorganized pharmaceutical retailers and customers is carried out in the present study to analyze the influence of organized retail and e-pharmacies on unorganized pharmaceutical retail sectors.
