## Abstract / Description
The present study develops three forecasting models for economy-wide energy intensity forecasting in Indian context. The developed models include a combined multivariate time series regression-Holt's smoothing model, an ARIMA model and a causal system dynamics model for energy intensity forecasting. The combined and system dynamics forecast models showed the energy price variables to be statistically insignificant in the forecasting of economy-wide energy intensity specific to Indian context. Even though all the three developed forecasting models gave satisfactory energy intensity forecast results, it was found that ARIMA model outperformed the combined forecast model and the causal system dynamics model with the least model error. The forecast results out of the three models indicate that the economy-wide energy intensity is expected to decline at a rate of 9.0 percent to 18.1 percent from 2017 to 2026. For efficient energy management through designing and implementing effective and useful energy related policies, the present study suggests the utilization of the ARIMA technique for capturing the economy-wide energy intensity forecasts in India.
