## Abstract / Description
Information Technology (IT) service disruptions are occurring more frequently in banking networks causing prolonged and significant break downs to banking services. In this study, using the idea of accumulated satisfaction, a unique optimization-simulation model is designed within the context of the UK retail banking environment. Agentbased modelling simulations are run to predict fluctuations in a bank's post-disruption market share based on the predisruption level of satisfaction for minor and major disruptions. Results from the simulation experiments show that most banks do not regain their pre-disruption market share. Further, higher the pre-disruption market share, higher will be the immediate reduction in market share and lower will be the probability of regaining the previous market share levels. However, higher the pre-disruption customer satisfaction, lower will be the immediate loss of market share and higher the probability of regaining market share. Interestingly a bank's brand loyalty among customers do not have any impact on the reduction in post-disruption market share nor the probability of regaining market share after the IT disruption.
