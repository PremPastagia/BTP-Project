## Abstract / Description
The research oriented manpower is instrumental in the growth of economies. Unfortunately, the growth of doctoral research community is not keeping pace with the growing research requirements of India. “Time” to complete the milestones during the doctoral tenure is considered as an important constraint in doctoral degree completion. The present study mainly focus on this ‘time’ factor to complete the milestones in a doctoral life cycle. Secondary data and data collected through a focus group questionnaire survey were utilized. The opinions out of the survey were pairwise compared and ranked utilizing the Thurstone scale. The five doctoral lifecycle milestones identified through focus group discussions were enrolment, registration, synopsis submission, thesis submission, and viva-voce examination.
