## Abstract / Description
The process of circular economy is gaining momentum in view of the ongoing nature depletion scenario in the world. The present study develops a co-flow econometric model on Cobb–Douglas aggregate production function framework for the circular economy process. The developed model is estimated using full information maximum likelihood estimation for the Indian copper industry. For the present systemic behaviour sustaining to future, the primary and secondary copper production was found to exhibit counterintuitive behaviour of “overshoot and collapse” beyond the year 2026. It is evidenced that the maximum environmental impact out of circular economy occurs in 2025 and maximum reduction of environmental impact occurs in 2029 in the copper industry.
