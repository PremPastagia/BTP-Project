INDEPENDENT STAGE 4 TARGET SPECIFICATION

Inputs: researcher-accepted restricted Stage 3 source intervals, with calendar
start inclusive/end exclusive. No producer algorithm or helper is used.

MONTH-END QUOTE
The quote at the end of the final calendar day is available only if exactly one
accepted interval covers that day and the calendar month is complete at cutoff.
An earlier missing day does not, by itself, invalidate an otherwise observed
month-end value. Any whole-month coverage restriction must therefore be explicit.

CALENDAR-DAY-WEIGHTED AVERAGE OF DAY-END QUOTES
For each interval, intersect its half-open date range with the calendar month.
Multiply its quoted price by the integer number of overlapping calendar days.
Divide the total price-days by the actual number of calendar days only when all
days are covered exactly once. Otherwise leave the average unavailable. Missing
days are never filled and the final interval is never extended. November2026 is
an incomplete calendar month at the explicit6November2026 cutoff.

CONTINUOUS-TIME SENSITIVITY ENVELOPE
The day-end quote average is a distinct estimand from actual intraday TWAP.
On a boundary day with previous-day quote q and new end-of-day quote p, an
unknown intraday transition can contribute any price-day area between min(q,p)
and max(q,p), given that the accepted path captures all price changes and no
unreported same-day round-trip changes occur. Adjust the interval-overlap area
at each boundary accordingly. Divide the lower and upper total areas by month
length. This creates a sensitivity envelope, not a confidence/prediction interval.

The preceding day must be known, including when the first covered day is the
first day of the month. If it is unknown, finite continuous-time bounds are not
certified even when all day-end quotes are observed. Date-only source headers do
not justify a midnight effective-time assumption. Known06:00 changes fit inside
the coarse envelope; the independent implementation deliberately does not claim
maximally tight bounds from partial intraday information.

INDEPENDENT CALCULATION
The implementation sums direct interval-overlap lengths using45-digit Decimal
arithmetic. The producer is expected to expand day-end quotes onto calendar days,
providing an alternative computation rather than sharing the original helper.

MATHEMATICAL FIXTURES (NOT RESEARCH OBSERVATIONS)
- February2024 has29days.
- Price10 for14days and20 for15days gives440/29.
- An uncertain10-to20 transition on15February gives bounds430/29 to440/29.
- A two-day gap leaves the average unavailable, even if month-end is known.
- An unknown preceding-day price leaves continuous-time bounds unavailable.
- Ending the final interval before month-end prevents quote extension.
- Duplicate/overlapping source intervals are rejected.
- November2026 remains incomplete and produces no finalized target.

No Stage 5 tests, forecasting models or inferential claims are included.
