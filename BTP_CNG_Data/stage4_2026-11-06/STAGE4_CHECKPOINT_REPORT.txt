METHODOLOGY AND TRANSFORMATIONS
1. Verify the accepted manifest and source identities.
2. Interpret accepted intervals at the researcher-approved day-end resolution.
3. Reject overlap and never bridge missing intervals or extend the final quote.
4. Derive month-end price only when the final calendar day is covered.
5. Derive a calendar-day-weighted mean only when every calendar day is covered.
6. Compute conditional timing envelopes from previous/current day-end quote extrema;
   withhold envelopes when the preceding-day price is unknown. These are sensitivity
   bounds under documentary-path assumptions, not confidence or prediction intervals.
7. Keep numerical observations at native grain and filter by documented release
   time before selecting any retained information state at a month-end origin.
8. Treat known national values at multiple origins/cities as information-state
   references, not new independent monthly measurements or city observations.
9. Exclude November 2025–October 2026 from price-based EDA. Quality/coverage checks
   still examine that window. Stage 6 must confirm the final test design and review
   prior-study exposure; this reservation is not automatically a pristine final test.
