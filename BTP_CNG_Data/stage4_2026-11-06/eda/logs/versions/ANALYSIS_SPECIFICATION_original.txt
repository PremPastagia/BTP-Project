STAGE 4 DESCRIPTIVE EXPLORATION SPECIFICATION

Scope
Researcher-accepted restricted source-based price paths for Delhi/Noida (IGL)
and Lucknow/Agra/Unnao (Green Gas). These are five named markets and two source
operators. They are not five independent market experiments. No model, forecast,
formal stationarity/break test, hypothesis verdict, or causal claim is planned.

Exploration and provisional reservation
The root target builder declares exploration_eligible. Filter on that declaration
before converting target values or computing descriptive statistics. Months from
November 2025 through October 2026 are provisionally reserved for potential final
evaluation and are excluded from every price, change, calendar and correlation
summary and plot. This is a provisional reservation, not a frozen Stage 6 design.
Coverage and missingness metadata may be counted for reserved months, without
displaying or summarizing target values. Incomplete November 2026 is not a target.

Questions
1. Which complete calendar months have full day-end coverage, month-end-only
   coverage or unavailable target definitions? Where do source gaps remain?
2. What are the levels, observed dispersion and changes of the available
   exploration-period targets? Report each target separately.
3. How often are consecutively observed month-end quotes unchanged? Do not compare
   across a missing month. Flat-price fractions are conditional on valid adjacent
   pairs and are not evidence of exhaustive event histories.
4. At contiguous accepted source boundaries, how many quotes change and how many
   remain identical? Missing-boundary jumps are not counted as known revisions.
5. How do observed calendar-month changes differ descriptively? Different years,
   regimes, coverage and shared operators can generate apparent patterns. No
   seasonality test or seasonal forecasting claim follows from this display.
6. How strongly do available price levels and adjacent-month changes co-move on
   exactly matched months? Report pair counts and operator overlap. Shared trends,
   common shocks and shared tariff decisions make correlations noncausal and
   unsuitable as evidence of forecast improvement.

Price definitions
Month-end is the accepted quote at the final calendar day. The other target is
the calendar-day-weighted average of day-end quotes. It is not an identified
continuous-time TWAP. Sensitivity envelopes, when supplied, are conditional
identification bounds, not confidence or prediction intervals.

Exploratory flags
Preserve every observation. Report nonpositive/nonfinite inputs as validation
failures. For nonzero adjacent month-end changes, an optional Tukey rule is only
an exploratory flag when a market has at least eight such changes and positive
IQR. Zero-IQR/too-small samples do not support that flagging rule. Also list
the largest absolute observed changes with context. No exclusion, winsorization,
interpolation or backward-fill follows from these flags.

Independent calculation check
Recompute headline coverage counts, valid adjacent pairs, exact flat-pair counts,
means and matched-month Pearson coefficients from original CSV strings using
stdlib date/Decimal/statistics arithmetic. Compare these with separate pandas
calculations under explicit numeric tolerance. No producer target aggregation
helper is imported. Target reconstruction itself is audited by a separate agent.

Outputs
Plain CSV descriptive tables, standalone PNG/SVG scientific figures, a factual
EDA report, input and output checksums, a consequential-experiment register,
independent arithmetic receipt and explicit limitations. Plot lines break at
missing months. No reserved prices appear in any figure or descriptive table.

Limits
No inferential p-values, effective-sample-size estimate, formal power calculation,
multivariate predictor relationship, sentiment feature, model selection or final
test score is produced. These are later-stage questions requiring their gates.
