CORRELATION AND CALENDAR INTERPRETATION
All coefficients use exactly matched exploratory months, with pair counts reported.
Level correlations can reflect common trends. Change correlations can reflect shared
operator tariff decisions and common shocks. Neither establishes a causal relationship
or additional forecasting value. No significance tests were performed.
Calendar displays mix different years/regimes and availability patterns. They do not
establish seasonality or justify a seasonal model. Five markets are not five independent
units: Delhi/Noida share IGL; Lucknow/Agra/Unnao share Green Gas.
