This approval covers qualified retrospective outcomes, not automatic historical
eligibility for numerical, text or lagged-price predictors.

Actual integration
- 566 market-month origins have an observed month-end target, across five markets.
- 54 origins are provisionally reserved for final evaluation; 512 are exploratory.
- 92 native records satisfy the implemented documented-release/source checks:
  64 quarterly GDP/PFCE records and 28 CNG-only national sales records.
- Six distinct semantic namespaces remain: constant/current GDP and PFCE, monthly
  CNG sales totals, and financial-year-to-date CNG sales totals.
- 124 as-of ledger rows reference 12 distinct native source observations across
  24 market-month origins. These are shared national factors, not 124 independent
  national measurements or city-specific demand observations.
- All 24 origins with any eligible numerical factor are in the reserved period
  (May–October 2026). ZERO of the 512 exploratory origins has a dated numerical
  factor under the present rules. The current information set therefore does
  not establish feasibility for an H1 comparison trained on exploratory history.

Exclusions
- 47,766 native numerical records have no publication date and were excluded.
  No one-month lag, annual-to-monthly expansion or retrieval-date substitution
  was used to manufacture historical availability.
- 28 station indicators remain excluded because flow versus operating stock is
  unresolved; station additions cannot be treated as station population.
- 395 unverified PPAC OCR/text transcriptions remain excluded.
- All 251 corporate metadata records remain ineligible as historical predictors.
  Exact raw-document bytes and fiscal/letter dates do not supply publication time.
- Retrospective retail target acceptance does not certify historical availability
  of lagged price inputs; the origin register explicitly retains this limitation.

As-of selection policy
1. Origin is the final instant of an Indian calendar month, only where an accepted
   observed month-end outcome exists. Missing targets do not create origins.
2. A native observation must have a documented release bound no later than the
   origin and a reference-period end no later than the origin date.
3. Group by semantic series, geography, native frequency and unit. Preserve
   constant/current-price definitions, fiscal-year-to-date versus monthly flow,
   and source vintages separately. Deduplicate exact source/version/native-period
   values using exact Decimal equality; preserve aliases and conflicting claims.
4. For each semantic group, select the latest eligible reference-period end,
   then the latest released version for that same reference period. Exclude tied
   versions with differing values. No conflicting ties occurred in this run.
5. Retain the original period, source hash, release basis and reference/release
   age. A held last-known value is a forecast information-state record, NEVER
   an invented monthly measurement. No national value is allocated to a city.

Publication evidence and qualification
The national-accounts PDF explicitly prohibits publication, telecast or internet
circulation until 4:00 PM on 5 June 2026. An independent Poppler extraction checked
all 64 GDP/PFCE cells against native table pages 15 and 17, the quarter/year layout
and crore units. This validates the particular release vintage; it does not make
the revised historical quarters available before June 2026.

CNG sales use documented PNGRB index upload dates, conservatively bounded by the
end of the listed Indian publication day. This is source-upload-date evidence,
not independent archived proof that identical bytes existed on that day. Every
as-of row exposes historical_vintage_status and publication_basis. Source-content
or upload-date revisions would require reopening the affected source eligibility.

H2 metadata feasibility
All 24 selected article records are retained in a timestamp-claim inventory.
22 have parseable timezone-aware publisher timestamp claims; one lacks a date
and another cannot be placed on the time axis unambiguously under these rules.
There are 5,820 origin/article metadata references whose timestamp claim precedes
the origin. This is NOT article-volume, sentiment, uncertainty or linguistic
feature engineering. Market relevance, syndication and full-text licence remain
unverified; all records say predictor_eligible=False. The corpus was selected for
research discovery and does not establish representative historical news coverage.
No corporate or text-based forecasting inputs were admitted.

Reserved evaluation protection
Mechanical integration covers all observed origins and explicitly marks the
provisional November 2025–October 2026 reservation. Only target presence/calendar
keys were used; no reserved outcome distributions, correlations, accuracy scores
or favorable model choices were calculated. No statistical tests or models ran.
Future evaluation design must account for sparse origins and information sets.

Independent verification
verify_asof_independent.py imports no producer functions. Twelve checks pass:
exhaustive reference-period/vintage selection, exact observed-origin membership,
month-end/reservation boundaries, source keys/hashes, unknown-date exclusions,
station/PPAC/corporate exclusions, a deliberately future-release negative control,
news metadata timing and accepted snapshot/target hash binding. It independently
recomputes every selected as-of record. This verifies specified calculations,
not every economic assertion or historical source-vintage claim.
