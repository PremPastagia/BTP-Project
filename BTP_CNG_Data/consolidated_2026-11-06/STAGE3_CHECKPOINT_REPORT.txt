METHOD
Reused byte-identical local sources; recovered missing public originals with bounded
HTTP requests; retained changed captures as new versions. Stored one object per
SHA256. Repaired PNGRB body/header schema variants from raw cells. Preserved source
dates at their actual precision. Consolidated numerical records at native frequency
and source/version grain with exact Decimal equality. Kept conflicting and unverified
claims outside canonical numerical observations. Deduplicated corporate documents
by raw hash, retaining all metadata aliases. No source assertions were silently
upgraded to verified historical truth or forecasting availability.

FAILURES AND CONTRADICTIONS
1. All 415 source-market coverage entries remain uncertified as complete price paths.
   Exact effective dates, city/GA boundaries and interval continuity require review.
2. Pune April 2023: MNGL chronology86 INR/kg versus Autocar 87 INR/kg. Both remain,
   and the disputed interval must not enter an accepted target path without resolution.
3. 11,576/11,699 price claims lack publication dates. Retrospective outcome-label
   validity differs from historical availability of lagged prices and predictors.
4. Corporate publication times remain unverified. Report years, letter dates,
   retrieval timestamps and HTTP last-modified dates are not inferred release dates.
5. Three MoPNG PDFs have malformed trailers: Poppler reads them, PyPDF fails.
   Originals are preserved and flagged for conditional use, not repaired silently.
6. An older IGL workbook contains 27 formulas and 17 nonpositive cached values.
   Six negative caches arise from formulas subtracting 2.5 from missing antecedents;
   these are not actual negative retail prices. Vintages are not silently spliced.
7. PDF recovery does not complete semantic extraction. The queue includes 301 PPAC
   reports, 108 gas notifications, 114 PNGRB reports, 185 corporate documents, 32 MoPNG
   policies, 17 operator/other histories and 4 other official/parliamentary documents.
8. Observed vehicle registrations/active stock, company procurement/sourcing histories,
   city demand, statutory tax/tariff histories and historical macro vintages remain
   partial or missing. National aggregates do not fill those city-level gaps.
