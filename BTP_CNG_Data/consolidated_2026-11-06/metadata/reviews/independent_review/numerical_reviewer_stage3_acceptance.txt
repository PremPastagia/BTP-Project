INDEPENDENT SCIENTIFIC STAGE 3 ACCEPTANCE REVIEW
Reviewer scope: price-source module and collection-level readiness. This is not
an independent review of my own numerical reconciliation implementation.

DECISION
Do not certify unconditional Stage 3 scientific acceptance yet. Repair and
collection work has materially improved the evidence, but the saved protocol's
price-path, availability, geographic-scope and researcher-approval gate is unmet.
Stage 4 cannot legitimately begin merely because engineering checks pass.

WHAT I VERIFIED INDEPENDENTLY
I read the actual structured price rows, components, coverage records,
corroboration records and source manifest. I did not import producer functions.
I recomputed canonical identifier uniqueness, positive price bounds, calendar
validity of parsed exact dates, and component-to-price foreign-key existence.
I separately parsed the original January 2026 PNGRB HTML with BeautifulSoup and
rechecked its SHA-256. The raw Dewas row starts with GAIL Gas Limited, has no GA-ID
body cell, and reports retail price96. The repaired record correctly identifies
GAIL, Dewas, Madhya Pradesh, blank GA-ID and INR96/kg. This independently supports
the documented layout correction for that sample; it is not source truth proof
for every observation. Detailed counts/checks are in the accompanying JSON.

PRICE-PATH GATE
The module contains 11,699 canonical price claims and 10,538 component rows.
All415 source-market coverage entries explicitly say complete_price_path_verified
is false. No final bounded market/operator price-path eligibility list has been
accepted. A sequence of snapshots or positive cells is not proof that all
intervening price changes were observed. Last source dates do not justify
extending prices indefinitely.

The source-date statuses retain 5,228 ambiguous/invalid numeric-date claims,
155 overnight windows,18 unspecified midnight labels,45 month-only claims,
12 unparsed labels and2 mid-month-only labels. These records may be useful
evidence, but exact daily intervals cannot be invented from them.
The producer also retains15 component arithmetic conflicts and12 unambiguous
revision-after-snapshot contradictions. They are not repaired facts.

Seven targeted crosscheck rows come from only two corroborating publications:
five market/effective-date agreements in one June2024 event, one Delhi price-level
agreement with unverified effective timing, and one Pune INR87versus86 conflict
with unverified timing. Five markets in one announcement do not provide five
independent event/regime validations, and these checks do not certify full
multi-operator histories. The parliamentary evidence is explicitly provisional
in its geographic extraction and must not be silently promoted to ground truth.

TIMING AND INFORMATION SET
11,576 of11,699 price claims have no recorded publication date. This is not by
itself a reason to discard retrospective outcome labels: later evidence can
verify historical outcomes. It is a reason not to assume the same retrospective
document was available as a predictor at an earlier forecast origin.
Every proposed predictor needs a defensible availability rule. An assumed
one-month lag, fiscal-year label, letter date or current download time cannot
replace a missing actual release history.

The current news table contains24 records; one has no publication date, and
all24 date claims are labelled publisher_claim_not_independent_archive_verification.
Corporate source recovery establishes exact document bytes, not historical
exchange/publication timestamps. A corpus with many PDFs does not automatically
support a matched historical H2 forecasting experiment.

DEMAND AND GEOGRAPHY
The56 new demand records include28 national CNG-only sales values and28 reported
station indicators. The station indicators are explicitly flagged as having an
unresolved flow-versus-stock definition. They cannot be treated as operating
station stock. National source totals cannot be allocated to cities without
evidence. Shared-operator cities and overlapping service-market labels require
an explicit dated mapping and a justified selection decision.

SYNTHETIC/UNSUPPORTED EVIDENCE
The collection preserves explicit source claims and exclusions rather than
restoring synthetic VAHAN estimates or unsafe legacy joins. Retaining legacy
quarantine copies is appropriate. I did not inspect every produced record for
authenticity and do not issue an all-data authenticity certificate. Remaining
source and semantic gaps must remain visible; source hashes alone cannot close
them.

REQUIRED RESEARCHER DECISIONS BEFORE ACCEPTANCE
1. Select explicit market/operator identities and bounded historical price
   intervals. State what source evidence establishes completeness and how
   independent corroboration will cover important changes and regimes.
2. Resolve or explicitly exclude the Pune discrepancy and ambiguous effective
   intervals. If uncertainty bounds are retained, accept those source bounds
   first; alternative target calculations belong in Stage4.
3. Define the historical information set. Exclude predictor observations with
   unsupported availability, recover real releases, or transparently restrict
   the study. Do not disguise ex-post inputs as real-time predictors.
4. Decide the feasible H2 window and source coverage, or document that H2 is
   presently inconclusive/infeasible. This decision must not assume improvement.
5. Approve factor-specific exclusions and stopping decisions for VAHAN,
   company procurement/sourcing, tax/tariff histories and other unresolved
   candidate factors. Failed requests alone do not prove irrecoverability.
6. Review the cumulative completion report and a versioned checksum-frozen
   candidate snapshot, then explicitly accept/revise/reject that snapshot.

POSSIBLE QUALIFIED WAY FORWARD
A deliberately restricted, well-documented subset could be accepted without
collecting every hypothetical factor. That requires explicit bounded scope,
source eligibility and honest exclusions. It does not justify declaring all
of Stage3scientifically complete on file volume alone. The final research
design still preserves both targets and all horizons, subject to later sample
feasibility checks. A restricted scope must be recorded as a researcher decision.

No Stage4target aggregation, EDA or modeling was performed for this review.
The review independently tests specified conditions and challenges acceptance;
it does not claim every historical source assertion was independently verified.
