NEWS FINDINGS AND RESOLVED AUDIT WARNINGS
24 source HTML hashes and publication fields were independently checked. No
current same-URL alternate-content-version loss was found.

An earlier structured timestamp contained an HTML-escaped timezone plus sign.
After the reviewer flagged it, the root producer normalized HTML entities while
preserving the original claim and date/time precision. Current output timestamps
parse successfully. Date-only claims remain explicitly time-unknown.

The root also changed article identity from URL-only to canonical URL plus raw
content hash. This retains future source versions instead of silently discarding
different bytes at one URL. Conflicting publisher date claims are now placed in
a review register rather than automatically treated as verified dates.

Remaining limitations include unverified historical publication instants, source
syndication, semantic duplicates, incomplete historical sampling and rights to
republish full texts. No sentiment, article-volume or collocation features were
generated. File integrity does not establish article representativeness.
