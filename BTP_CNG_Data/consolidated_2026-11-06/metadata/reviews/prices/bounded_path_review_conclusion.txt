RESULT
Useful bounded date-resolution source paths can be proposed for Delhi/Noida
(IGL) and Lucknow/Agra/Unnao (Green Gas). They provide two source operators,
but do not yet satisfy an unconditional accepted-data gate.

The review creates287 source interval claims. Twelve intervals abut independently

No month-end target, TWAP, monthly join or EDA was computed.

WHY THIS IS DIFFERENT FROM FILLING SPARSE SNAPSHOTS
IGL's worksheet explicitly calls its event column Date of Revision. GGL's primary
PDF declares Historical CNG prices from01.04.2019 onwards and labels Effective From.
Interpreting a quoted tariff as applying until the next listed tariff event is
supported by these chronology definitions. The ledger does not infer persistence
from a PNGRB monthly snapshot or fill an unexplained missing market cell.

IGL intervals use consecutive actual worksheet rows only, with positive quotes
for the same market at both boundaries. A blank, zero, dash or invalid source
boundary breaks the interval. No interpolation bridges such breaks. GGL intervals
start after explicit sales commencement and preserve its Lucknow24July2026 footnote.
Neither operator's final quote is extended beyond a subsequent source boundary.

ACTUAL GATE CONSTRAINTS
- Date-only event headers do not prove prices changed at midnight. New primary
  reporting explicitly gives06:00 changes. A true continuous-time monthly TWAP
  requires the relevant intraday times, or an explicitly approved date-resolution
  estimand plus uncertainty/sensitivity treatment. The ledger assumes no midnight.
- Unknown chronology publication dates remain unknown. A later workbook can
  support retrospective label reconstruction but not establish that every lagged
  price/predictor was available at a historical forecasting origin.
- Documentary chronology completeness is a source assertion, not independent
  verification that every revision was recorded. Selected event corroboration
  is not an audit of every interval.
- IGL source gaps remain. GGL known timing disagreements remain. Excluding Pune
  avoids its86-versus87 price dispute, but does not resolve these other issues.
- Multiple nearby cities serviced by one operator must not be treated as fully
  independent price experiments; effective sample size remains a later assessment.

SCIENTIFICALLY DEFENSIBLE NEXT DECISION
The researcher could review a restricted source-based, date-resolution study with
explicit interval exclusions and uncertainty, retaining H1/H2 feasibility limits.
That is a transparent scope/estimand qualification requiring explicit acceptance.
It must not be represented as a fully verified continuous-time historical path.
Without that qualification or further event evidence, the unconditional Stage3
price-acceptance gate remains unsatisfied. This review does not approve Stage4.
