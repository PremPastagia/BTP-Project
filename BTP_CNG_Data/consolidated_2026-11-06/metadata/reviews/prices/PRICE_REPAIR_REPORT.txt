CRITICAL PNGRB REPAIR
The original January 2026 and December 2025 HTML tables have a GA-ID header but
omit that cell from every body row, appending a blank final cell. This source
layout defect shifts operator, geography, state, date, retail price and components.
1,208 rows were repaired. The previous audit's 1,207 count missed one row because
its revision-date cell was blank rather than a date string.

The repair inserts an explicitly blank GA ID; no ID is inferred from another month.
For example, January 2026 Dewas/GAIL/Madhya Pradesh is INR96/kg, rather than the
INR55.48/kg gas-cost cell previously misclassified as retail price. Independent
verification also caught the missing-date Bastar row, whose retail INR87.71/kg
had previously been mistaken for its INR65.60/kg component.

Every table month is checked against the actual source heading, not inferred
solely from table position. Original table headers and body layout classifications
are retained in metadata/pngrb_table_schema.csv.
